EventFlow style guide

Four directions for the redesign. A to C keep the botanical brand: green, brass, ivory, Playfair Display and Inter. C sits between A and B. D is a dark, dashboard-style take on DaVinci's black and white. Pick one, and Phase 2 turns it into css/tokens.css.

Ledger

A calm, paper-like workspace. An ivory page with white working surfaces, and thin warm rules instead of shadows. Green is the ink for navigation and main actions. Brass appears only where money or selection is involved. Playfair is kept for page titles and totals, so a proposal's grand total reads like the bottom line of a florist's ledger.

Where it's bold
The proposal total, set large in Playfair over a brass rule.
Good for
Long working sessions in dense tables. It stays quiet on a phone.
Trade-off
Less memorable at first glance. It relies on typography and spacing to look premium.

Shared fixes

Applies to every direction. Some of today's colors fail WCAG AA (4.5:1 for small text), so Phase 2 tokens will use these instead.

Draft badge text #8A8374 is 3.19:1 on its fill. Use #6B6455 (4.98:1).
"Viewed" badge text #A6742D is 3.52:1. Use #8A6420 (4.63:1).
Approved badge text #2F7D5A is 4.35:1. Use #28704F (5.19:1).
Brass text on ivory #9C6B1F is 4.33:1 on #FAF7F2. Use brass text on white only, or #86591A on ivory (5.68:1).
Placeholder-grey text #9C9686 is 2.95:1 on white. Keep it for decoration only. Readable text uses #6B6455.
Brass on dark green #9C6B1F is too dark there. Use #D9B26F (5.99:1) on dark green.
Input borders #D3CBB8 is 1.61:1 on white, under the 3:1 a field edge needs. Use #958C7A (3.33:1). C already does. A and B would adopt it too.
Text on brass (C) White on #A57C2E is 3.80:1. Brass buttons use #7A5A1C (6.35:1). #A57C2E stays for rules, borders and markers, which need 3:1.
